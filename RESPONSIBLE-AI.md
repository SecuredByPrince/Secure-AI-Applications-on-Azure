# Responsible AI

## Overview

This document defines the Responsible AI principles, governance considerations, operational controls, and AI safety practices implemented within the Secure AI Applications on Azure project.

The purpose of this project is not only to secure AI systems technically, but also to demonstrate how enterprise AI workloads can be governed responsibly using layered operational, governance, and security controls.

Modern AI systems introduce risks beyond traditional cybersecurity concerns.

Enterprise AI systems may generate:
- unsafe outputs
- harmful content
- misleading responses
- biased recommendations
- privacy concerns
- governance failures
- operational misuse scenarios

As AI adoption accelerates, organisations increasingly require:
- accountability
- transparency
- oversight
- governance maturity
- operational visibility
- AI risk management

This project integrates Responsible AI principles into the overall AI security architecture to demonstrate how governance and security can operate together inside modern enterprise AI environments.

# Why Responsible AI Matters

AI systems increasingly influence:
- business decisions
- operational workflows
- customer interactions
- productivity systems
- analytics processes
- enterprise automation

Unlike traditional software systems, AI systems dynamically generate responses based on:
- prompts
- contextual instructions
- training behaviour
- inference logic
- model interactions

This introduces new governance and trust challenges.

Without proper oversight, AI systems may:
- generate harmful content
- expose sensitive data
- produce biased outputs
- generate misleading responses
- violate policy requirements
- operate without accountability

Responsible AI helps organisations reduce these risks operationally.
