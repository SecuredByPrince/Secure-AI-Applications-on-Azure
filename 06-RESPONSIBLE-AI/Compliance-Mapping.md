# Compliance Mapping

## Overview

This document defines the compliance alignment strategy, governance mapping model, operational control alignment, regulatory readiness framework, and Responsible AI governance mappings implemented within the Secure AI Applications on Azure project.

Enterprise AI systems increasingly operate within environments requiring:
- governance accountability
- operational transparency
- behavioural monitoring
- auditability
- security controls
- risk management
- Responsible AI oversight

Large Language Models (LLMs) and generative AI systems introduce governance and operational risks involving:
- unsafe outputs
- behavioural inconsistencies
- adversarial manipulation
- information disclosure
- governance violations
- accountability gaps
- operational misuse

Unlike traditional software systems, AI systems:
- generate probabilistic outputs
- rely on contextual interpretation
- adapt dynamically to prompts
- introduce behavioural uncertainty
- require continuous governance oversight

Without governance and compliance alignment, organizations may struggle to:
- demonstrate operational accountability
- validate AI safeguards
- support audit readiness
- maintain regulatory alignment
- establish governance maturity
- support trustworthy AI adoption

This project demonstrates how enterprise AI systems can improve governance readiness through:
- compliance alignment
- operational transparency
- behavioural monitoring
- governance accountability
- Responsible AI controls
- auditability mechanisms
- layered security architecture

The implementation aligns operational controls with:
- ISO/IEC 42001
- NIST AI RMF
- Microsoft Responsible AI Standard
- MITRE ATLAS
- CIS Controls
- Zero Trust principles

The environment prioritizes:
- governance maturity
- operational transparency
- behavioural accountability
- audit readiness
- Responsible AI alignment
- enterprise AI resilience

# Why Compliance Mapping Matters

Traditional compliance models generally focus on:
- infrastructure security
- access management
- network protections
- operational governance

Enterprise AI systems introduce additional governance complexity because:
- AI behaviour is probabilistic
- contextual interpretation changes dynamically
- adversarial manipulation is possible
- behavioural risks evolve continuously

Without compliance alignment:
- governance gaps may emerge
- operational trust may decline
- accountability visibility may weaken
- audit readiness may become difficult

Compliance mapping improves:
- governance maturity
- operational accountability
- behavioural visibility
- AI operational resilience
