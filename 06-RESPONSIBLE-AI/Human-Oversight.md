# Human Oversight

## Overview

This document defines the human oversight framework, governance accountability model, operational review processes, behavioural validation workflows, and decision escalation procedures implemented within the Secure AI Applications on Azure project.

Enterprise AI systems increasingly influence:
- operational workflows
- business decisions
- knowledge retrieval
- automated recommendations
- customer interactions
- security operations
- governance processes

Large Language Models (LLMs) and generative AI systems introduce behavioural complexity because outputs are:
- probabilistic
- context-dependent
- dynamically generated
- influenced by prompts and orchestration logic

Unlike traditional deterministic software systems, AI systems may:
- produce inaccurate outputs
- generate unsafe recommendations
- exhibit behavioural inconsistencies
- amplify bias
- respond unpredictably
- be manipulated adversarially

Without human oversight, organizations may fail to:
- identify harmful outputs
- validate behavioural integrity
- detect governance violations
- maintain accountability
- ensure operational trust
- manage AI-related incidents effectively

This project demonstrates how enterprise AI environments can improve operational resilience through:
- human-in-the-loop validation
- governance accountability
- operational review processes
- behavioural monitoring
- escalation workflows
- Responsible AI controls

The implementation integrates human oversight controls with:
- Azure AI Foundry
- Azure OpenAI Service
- Azure AI Content Safety
- Prompt Shields
- Microsoft Sentinel
- governance monitoring
- behavioural telemetry analytics

The environment prioritizes:
- accountability
- operational trust
- behavioural safety
- governance maturity
- transparency
- enterprise AI resilience

# Why Human Oversight Matters

Traditional software systems generally operate through:
- deterministic workflows
- predictable outputs
- explicit logic

Enterprise AI systems behave differently because:
- outputs are probabilistic
- contextual interpretation varies
- prompts influence behaviour
- adversarial manipulation is possible

Without human oversight:
- harmful outputs may go unnoticed
- governance violations may increase
- operational trust may decline
- accountability gaps may emerge

Human oversight improves:
- behavioural validation
- governance maturity
- operational transparency
- AI resilience
