# NIST AI RMF Mapping

## Overview

This document maps the Secure AI Applications on Azure project to the principles and operational guidance defined in the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF).

The purpose of this mapping is to demonstrate how enterprise AI security engineering, monitoring, governance, and Responsible AI controls can conceptually align with NIST AI RMF operational practices.

This project is not intended to represent formal NIST compliance certification.

Instead, it demonstrates practical implementation concepts using:
- Azure AI Foundry
- Azure OpenAI Service
- Microsoft Sentinel
- Azure Monitor
- Prompt Shields
- Azure AI Content Safety
- AI threat monitoring
- governance workflows
- operational security controls

The project prioritizes:
- operational visibility
- governance accountability
- AI safety readiness
- behavioural monitoring
- operational resilience
- Responsible AI practices

# Why NIST AI RMF Matters

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework provides guidance for:
- trustworthy AI
- operational governance
- AI risk management
- behavioural oversight
- monitoring visibility

- operational resilience

Enterprise AI systems introduce operational and behavioural risks involving:
- adversarial manipulation
- unsafe outputs
- prompt injection
- behavioural inconsistency
- operational misuse
- governance failures
- monitoring blind spots

The framework helps organizations:
- manage AI risks
- improve governance maturity
- improve operational trust
- strengthen AI resilience
